Education → Bedrock

Turn a Minecraft Education world (.mceditworld or .mcworld) into a world Minecraft Bedrock can open. It all happens on your device: nothing is uploaded.

Options

How to open your converted world

Windows / Mac: double-click the downloaded .mcworld file and Minecraft Bedrock imports it. iPad / iPhone / Android: open the file from your Files or Downloads app and choose Minecraft.

Your original file is never changed. Keep it as a backup.

What this does, and what it cannot do

Bedrock refuses worlds that are marked as Education worlds. This app clears those Education marks in the world’s settings file (level.dat) and repackages the world as a .mcworld. Every other file in the world is copied across unchanged.

Education-only things (chalkboards, allow/deny/border blocks, cameras, Code Builder and similar) do not exist in regular Bedrock, so they can show up as missing or blank blocks. Your Bedrock must also be the same version as the Education app that last saved the world, or newer. I could not test against every world. Locked lesson worlds from the Education library are encrypted and cannot be converted.
